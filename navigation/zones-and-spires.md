# Zones and Ancient Spires

The big picture: which zone comes next, and where its fast-travel Spire is.
Checked against Enshrouded **1.0** guides (October to November 2026). ⚠️ means the sources disagree.

## How fast travel works

- **Ancient Spires** are the tall puzzle towers. Climb to the top and talk to the Flame. The Spire becomes a permanent fast-travel point, and nearby Flame Shrines, Sanctums and Elixir Wells show up on your map.
- **Flame Altars** are the ones you build. Every altar you place is also a fast-travel point, so drop one near anywhere you'll come back to.
- Use the map and pick the Spire or Altar marker to travel.

## Tips

- Do each zone's Spire first. It unlocks fast travel and shows you where the Sparks are.
- Collect **Sparks** from Flame Shrines and Sanctums. You need them to strengthen the Flame, which raises your Shroud time and opens the next zones.
