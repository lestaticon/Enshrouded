# Resources by zone

Where to farm what. "Shroud" means you'll find it inside the Shroud in that zone, so keep an eye on your timer.
Checked against Enshrouded **1.0** guides (October to November 2026). ⚠️ means the sources disagree or only one source names the spot.

## Springlands (level 3 to 9)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| Flintstone | Small Flint Mine, north of Braelyn Bridge |
| Salt | Saline Quarry (east of the Cinder Vault) or Egerton Salt Mines (Hunter's quest) |
| Shroud Liquid, Shroud Spores, Active Mycelium | Springlands Shroud, from Fell enemies and plants |
| Metal Scraps | Old machinery and loot around scavenger camps |
| Wood, Resin, Plant Fiber, Twigs | Everywhere. Resin comes mostly from stumps and red-leaved trees. |

## Revelwood (level 11 to 15)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| **Copper Ore** | **Mark of Sameth** (best, and the Crucible is here), Mineshafts just north of it, or Glimmer Rock Mine |
| Clay | Glimmer Rock Mine and around Revelwood |
| Hardwood | Revelwood trees |
| Flax, Chamomile, Indigo, Moss | Around Revelwood |
| Amber | Revelwood Shroud |

## Blackmire (off Revelwood)

Foliage, Mushroom Flesh, Reptile Hide, Vukah Nesting Material. No named mines.

## Nomad Highlands (level 16 to 20)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| **Tin Ore** | **Mining Rift** (the upper deposits sit above the Shroud ⚠️) or Smothering Pit, south of Vukah Outpost |
| Limestone, Straw, Wheat | Around the Highlands |
| Calla Luna | At night |
| Feathers | Highlands and Kindlewastes |

## Kindlewastes (level 21 to 27)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| **Iron Ore** | **Ridgeback Mine**, north-east of Halls of Founders. It's in the Shroud, guarded by a Fell boss, and needs Shroud Passage level 5. |
| Sulfur, Lapislazuli | Ternion Mine, east of the Draconian Vulture Nest |
| Sand, Fine Sand, Sandstone, Glass Shards | Desert areas |
| Palm Wood, Aloe, Saffron | Around the oases |

## Albaneve Summits (level 28 to 35)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| **Silver Ore** | Albaneve Shroud ⚠️ (no named mine yet) |
| Obsidian | Obsidian Mine, north-east of Forge of Obsidia |
| Red Marble | Camellia Lake Marble Quarry (south of Rothstep) or Fletcher Marble Quarry |
| Coal, Amethyst | Near Fletcher Quarry and the Obsidian Mine |
| Conifer Logs, Raw Wool, Ice | Around the Summits |

## Veilwater Basin (level 37 to 45)

| Resource | Best spot |
|---|---|
| **Gold Ore** | Underwater. Some is near the Aquamarine Mine ⚠️ |
| Aquamarine | Aquamarine Mine, south-east of Dewdrop Farm |
| Pearl, Mother-of-Pearl | Underwater |
| Bamboo, Tropical Wood, Coffee Beans, Rice | Around the Basin |

## Cinder's Cradle (new in 1.0, north of Veilwater)

Celestium Ore (Celestium Mining Site ⚠️), Basalt, Moonstone, Magma Gel and Cinder Rock. Archaic Shards drop from Ancient Constructs. Diamonds are in the Shroud.

## Anywhere

Shroud Wood (dead trees in the Shroud), Paper Reed (lake edges), Luminous Growth (caves) and Sparks (Flame Shrines and Sanctums).

> Mined ground grows back after about 30 minutes or a world restart, unless it's inside your Flame Altar area ⚠️.

Sources: [allthings.how materials by region](https://allthings.how/enshrouded-1-0-materials-by-region-where-to-find-each-one/), [banrado ore farming](https://banrado.de/en/enshrouded-ore-farming), Steam community mine-locations guide (mine names; that guide has since been taken down, so treat its spots as unconfirmed).
