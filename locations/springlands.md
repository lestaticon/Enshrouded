# Springlands (and the Cinder Vault start)

> **Version:** Enshrouded 1.0 (released 15 October 2026 on PC and PS5).
> **Status:** First draft, compiled 21 November 2026 from community sources (listed at the bottom). Not yet checked in-game.
> **Legend:** ⚠️ (and `<!-- VERIFY -->`) = sources disagree or the detail could not be confirmed. Distances are approximate and taken from community maps.

1.0 reworked the Springlands environments, early-game enemy camps and crafting recipes, and added chests and new areas near the map edges. Guides written for Early Access (before October 2026) may be out of date. [1]

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Biome level | 1 to 10 |
| Flame Level needed | 1 to 2 |
| Neighbours | Revelwood (north), Nomad Highlands (east) |
| Sub-area | Low Meadows (far east) |
| Main enemies | Wolves, Fell (Shroud), Scavengers (Rookmore, Wescott), Vukah (Fort Kelvin, Vukah Ceremony Hill), Hollow (Necropolis, Hollow Halls) |
| Bosses | Fell Thunderbrute, Direwolf, Fell Striker, Scavenger Gorger Matron, Vukah Brawler |

Sources: [2], [3]

## 1. Getting out of the Cinder Vault

1. **Wake up** in a capsule in the Cinder Vault and commune with the Flame. This gives you the **Flame Altar** recipe and starts the quest **Reach The Plains**. Commune again before leaving for the **Rested** buff. [4]
2. **Starter cave loot**, in rough order [4]:
   - Bandages on a ruined platform, and a chest with a Torch.
   - Wooden crates give Wood Logs and unlock the **Campfire** recipe.
   - **Explosive Powder Balls** on a ledge. Throw one at the cracked rubble wall to reach a chest with the **Hunter's Ring**. ⚠️ Another guide puts a Hunter's Ring about 190 m south-west of Longkeep instead. [3]
   - A **Rusty Sword** stuck in a wooden platform.
   - **Torn Cloth** from Scavengers unlocks the **Tiny Backpack** and **Grappling Hook** recipes.
   - In the water cave: **Metal Scraps** on the pool floor and an underwater chest with the **Ragged Shirt**.
3. **Leave** by blowing up the collapsed exit wall with a second powder ball. A **Return Beacon** outside lets you fast-travel back to **Longkeep**. [4]
4. **Nearby extras:** Shroud Fell drop a **Fell Defender Shield**, and a boarded doorway (needs an axe) hides the **Lesser Ring of Stamina**. [4]

**Shroud timer:** entering the Shroud starts a 5-minute timer on default settings. Leave before it runs out. [4]

## 2. Your first base

- **Flame Altar:** costs 5 Stone. Place it on open ground outside the Shroud. It claims a 40 × 40 × 40 m building area. In 1.0, your **first level-up now happens when you place it**. [1], [4]
- **Workbench:** place it inside the altar area. It unlocks building, furniture and tool recipes, and repairs gear for free. [4]
- **Shelter:** a small roofed room with a bed, a fire and storage keeps **Rested** going past ten minutes. [4]
- **First tools:** Axe and Pickaxe each need Twigs, Stone and String. The Bow needs 5 Twigs and 1 String. Wooden Arrows are made from Twigs. [4]

### Strengthening the Flame to level 2

Needs **1 Spark, 5 Resin, 5 Penny Bun, 5 Bones, 5 Shroud Liquid, 5 Animal Fur**. No boss trophy is needed for this first upgrade. Later levels need boss heads. [4]

**Waking Oswald:** clear the guards at his vault and climb to the upper balcony for bombs and lore. Search the rubble behind his chamber for the **Hunter's Bow**, then use the glowing Cinder Vessel to wake him. He unlocks the Charcoal Kiln, Forge and Scrappy Pickaxe, and upgrades gear with Runes. [4]

## 4. First boss: Fell Thunderbrute

- **Quest:** Clear The Elixir Well (from the Blacksmith).
- **Where:** an Elixir Well in a shrouded ravine. ⚠️ One guide says near Braelyn Bridge [4]. Another says about 430 m south-east of the Springlands Ancient Spire, or about 280 m south of Small Flint Mine [3]. These may be the same well.
- **Getting there:** level 3 Fell dual-wielders and archers on wooden scaffolds. A **Misfortune Mace** chest is at the top of the tower, and an **Ice Bolt** spell charge is in the pit below.
- **Fight:** open with the bow, dodge the cleaves and punish with fast strikes. Dodge *through* its leaping ground pound, not away from it. Fur armor is recommended.
- **Drops:** the **Fell Thunderbrute Head** and a **Shroud Core** are guaranteed. The Shroud Core upgrades the Flame Altar to level 2, growing the build area from 40 m to 80 m per side. ⚠️ The chance drop is named Thunderbrute Greatsword in one guide and Scrappy Greatsword in another.
- **Afterwards:** chop the red, pulsing **Shroud Root** for skill points. A cleared Elixir Well gives 3 points and a standalone root gives 1.

Sources: [3], [4], [5]

## 5. Ancient Spires

Climbing a Spire and speaking to the Flame at the top unlocks a permanent fast-travel point and reveals nearby Flame Sanctums. [4], [6]

| Spire | Where |
|---|---|
| Ancient Spire – Springlands | Middle-west of the region. The Blacksmith's quest **The Ancient Spires** sends you here. |
| Ancient Spire – Low Meadows | About 1,460 m east of the Springlands Spire |

Source: [3]

**Springlands Spire, floor by floor** [4]:
1. A teleport pad and wall switches. Loot: Scorching Wand, Makeshift Daggers, Shepherd's Lightning Staff.
2. Spike traps and buttons you shoot with the bow. Loot: Scout Hood.
3. Lava platforms and high switches. Gold chest: **Greatsword of the Old Order**.

**Ancient Obelisks** ("In A World Destined For Ruin", "The Flame Touched Face") reveal hidden Flame Shrines and Shroud Roots. [2], [6]

## 6. Flame Shrines and Flame Sanctums

Both give **Sparks**, which you spend at your Flame Altar to Strengthen the Flame. A Shrine is a flame bowl you collect from. A Sanctum is a small dungeon with the Spark at the end. [6]

## 7. Key resources and where to find them

| Resource | Where | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Plant Fiber, Twigs | Bushes everywhere | String, tools, arrows |
| Wood Logs, Resin | Trees | Building, Flame level 2 |
| Stone, Dirt | Mining anywhere | Flame Altar, tools |
| Flintstone | Flint nodes; Small Flint Mine | Early tools; good mining XP |
| Salt | Egerton Salt Mines (about 600 m north-west of the Springlands Spire); ⚠️ probably also Saline Quarry | Cooking ⚠️ |
| Penny Bun | Mushrooms in woods | Flame level 2 (×5) |
| Purple Berries | Leafy bushes near boulders | Early food |
| Animal Fur, Bones, Raw Wolf Meat | Wolves | Armor, Glider, Flame level 2, food |
| Shroud Wood, Shroud Spores, Shroud Liquid | Inside the Shroud, Fell enemies, Elixir Wells | Glider, Grappling Hook, Flame level 2 |
| Metal Scraps | Starter water cave; Shroud pockets | Grappling Hook |
| Honey, Wax | Gathered in the wild ⚠️ (exact spots not listed) | Honey is food (+30 stamina regen) |
| Fire Fly, Midnight Lily, Fresh Shroud Spore | Night only | Fishing bait, alchemy |
| Gold coins | Loot chests (unlocked early in 1.0) | Vanity and trading recipes |

Copper (Revelwood) and Tin (Nomad Highlands) are **not** in the Springlands. You'll need them to move on.

Sources: [2], [3], [4]

**Two early traversal unlocks:**
- **Glider:** 8 Shroud Wood, 2 Animal Fur, 2 String, 2 Shroud Spores.
- **Grappling Hook:** 4 Metal Scraps, 7 String, 10 Shroud Spores. It only works on grapple points.

Source: [4]

## 8. Places and notable loot

**Landmarks:**
- **Longkeep:** ruins right outside the start, with a Return Beacon.
- **Braelyn Bridge:** ruins near the Elixir Well and the Blacksmith's vault.
- **Fort Kelvin:** a Vukah-held settlement, near Hannah Reed and Elio Ricci.
- **Woodgard:** church chest, farmer's buried chest, and the Noble armor set about 20 m east.

**Other places:**
- Dungeons: Mistbury Catacombs, Necropolis, Temple Ruin, Hidden Tomb, Hidden Tomb (Low Meadows), Springlands Hollow Halls.
- Elixir Wells: east of Braelyn Bridge, east of Woodgard, west of Ferndale.
- Shroud Roots: south of Peaceful Acres, north-west of Woodgard, east of Small Flint Mine, east of Saline Quarry, Ring of Rot, Fox Chase.

Sources: [2], [3]

**Fixed-location gear** [3], [4]:
- **Tainted Axe** (Epic): chest under a Braelyn Bridge pylon. Open it with one powder ball.
- **Pikemead's Bulwark** (Legendary shield): about 150 m west of Mistbury Catacombs.
- **Wailing Blade** (Legendary sword): Scavenger Stash.
- **Reaper's Wand** (Epic): Fox Chase.
- **Staff of the Serpent** (Epic): silver chest in the basement at Netherton.
- **Wanderer's Boots** (Epic): about 120 m south-east of Scavenger Stash.
- **Flame Pact** armor set: about 70 m north of Temple Ruin.
