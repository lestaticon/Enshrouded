# Crafting unlock order

What to unlock, in roughly what order. Checked against Enshrouded **1.0** guides (October to November 2026). ⚠️ means the sources disagree.

## 1. The basics (first hour)

1. **Flame Altar** (5 Stone): you get it by communing with the Flame in the Cinder Vault where you start. Placing it gives your first level-up.
2. **Workbench** (8 Wood Logs, 3 String): place it inside the altar area. It gives you building recipes, tools and free repairs.
3. **First tools:** Axe, Pickaxe and Bow, from Twigs, Plant Fiber, Stone and String.

## 2. Rescue the craftspeople

Each craftsperson sleeps in an **Ancient Vault** in Springlands. Wake them, craft a **Summoning Staff** (1 Twig) and place them at your base. Each one unlocks a new set of stations.

| Order | Who | Where | Needs first | Key unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blacksmith** (Oswald Anders) | Ancient Vault just south of the Springlands Spire | Nobody | Charcoal Kiln, Forge, Smelter and Crucible (copper bars), Glider, Grappling Hook, later Blast Furnace and Smithing Tools |
| 2 | **Hunter** (Athalan Skree) | Ancient Vault east of the Springlands Spire ⚠️ (one guide says Low Meadows) | Nobody | Hand Spindle, Tanning Station, Loom, Spinning Wheel, Diving Helmet |
| 3 | **Alchemist** (Balthazar) | Ancient Vault west of the Springlands Spire | Nobody | Mortar, Mill, potions, Improved Grappling Hook |
| 4 | **Farmer** (Emily Fray) | Ancient Vault north of the Springlands Spire | Hunter's quest | Cooking Station, Kitchen, seeds, Beehive Smoker |
| 5 | **Carpenter** (Cade Hawthorn) | Ancient Vault in the Shroud, Springlands or Low Meadows ⚠️. Break the wall with a pickaxe. | Alchemist's quest | Table Saw, Masonry Tools, Sparkworks Workbench, Advanced Glider |
| 6 | **Collector** (Alden Crowley) | Hollow Halls, Springlands (around level 13 to 17) | Alchemist's quest ⚠️ (one guide says Farmer and Carpenter too) | Ectoplasm Press |
| 7 | **Bard** (Valory) | Toward the Blackmire, off Revelwood ⚠️ | Nobody | Bard's daggers |
| Later | **Ancient Researcher** (Loraine Crim) | Cinder's Cradle | Main story | Infusion Altar, Gem Forge |

**Easy route:** Blacksmith, then Hunter, then Alchemist, then Farmer and Carpenter as their quests appear.

## 3. Metal tiers (what to mine next)

Each tier needs the zone that has its ore, so the metal ladder is also the route through the map.

| Tier | Ore | Zone | Station |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scrap | Metal Scraps | Springlands (loot, old machinery) | Forge (Metal Sheets) |
| Copper | Copper Ore | Revelwood | Smelter + Crucible |
| Bronze | Copper + Tin | Tin is in the Nomad Highlands | Smelter |
| Iron | Iron Ore | Kindlewastes (in the Shroud) | Blast Furnace ⚠️ |
| Silver | Silver Ore | Albaneve Summits (in the Shroud) | Blast Furnace ⚠️ |
| Gold | Gold Ore | Veilwater Basin (underwater) | Needs a Mining Sieve ⚠️ |
| Celestium | Celestium Ore | Cinder's Cradle | ⚠️ not confirmed |

## 4. Strengthening the Flame

Spend **Sparks** and materials at the Flame Altar to raise the Flame level. Each level gives you more Shroud time and a bigger build area, and opens the next zones. Later levels need a boss trophy (for example the Fell Thunderbrute's head). ⚠️ Our sources don't list every level's cost yet.
